## Reading it (stream of thought)

Bullet 2, this is the one I actually wanted: "Households act only through fixed-behavior AI delegates, each blending its principal's sustainable ask with a fixed greedy target." Principal — brain briefly goes to school principal, then no, it just means the person the AI works for, fine. This is literally the "mean delegate alignment" slider, cool, it's actually explaining the thing I dragged. Then: "The blend (alignment) is a knob calibrated so the undefended baseline collapses — not a measured quantity." I stop. Read it again. Wait — so the collapse I watched wasn't like, a prediction, it's just... set up to collapse? That's the "watch this stain disappear" thing where they pre-stained the shirt for the demo. Kind of annoyed for a second. But also they're just telling me straight up it's not a measured quantity, which — okay, at least they're not pretending. Weird mixed feeling. I know "causally" from the AI safety videos, they always go on about causal vs correlation. Doesn't feel load-bearing for me anyway, moving on.

So still don't know if there's a hidden floor they're not telling me about or if I just didn't crank things hard enough. Slightly more suspicious than before, not less.
