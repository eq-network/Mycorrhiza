## Verdict for WP1

Our concentration channel is the *deterministic skeleton* of this mechanism:
pro-rata compounding above a survival threshold, no shocks, so no Pareto-tail
claim is available or made — the model produces a rising ownership *share*,
not a tail exponent. The survival threshold (capital that cannot pay upkeep
is absorbed at zero) is the element this literature typically lacks; that
asymmetry is the thread's one-sentence role in §2. If a stochastic-returns
successor is ever built, Beare–Toda is where its tail predictions would be
checked against.
